What you will do

  1. Confirm your device and match the line to a card.
  2. Try 6 practice attempts.
  3. Complete 6 short blocks (90 recorded attempts).

Your privacy

Nothing is uploaded. Your browser downloads one anonymous JSON file for you to send to the researcher.

Step 2 of 2 · Match the screen size

Place the short edge of a standard credit/debit card against the line. Move the slider until both ends match.

Before you start

Sit comfortably and use only the selected device. Use the filled target marked TAP. After every attempt, even a miss, aim for the newly marked target on the other side. Do not retry the previous side.

Keep the page open. Take breaks only before the first tap of a new block. Use an actual trackpad if you select Trackpad; the browser cannot distinguish it from a mouse.

0 of 90

Tap the filled target marked TAP. A successful first tap starts the block and is not counted. Then switch sides after every attempt, even a miss.

Practice complete

During recording, keep switching sides even after a miss. Breaks belong between blocks, before the first tap.
